Invited by the EFCC, the DSS or the Police?

An invitation is not a conviction, and honouring one does not mean facing it alone. This is what to do while you arrange counsel.

STEP 1

Do not go alone

You are entitled to have a lawyer present. Ask for the invitation in writing, and note the name of the officer and the office issuing it.

STEP 2

Say nothing you have not read

You are not obliged to make or sign a statement without counsel. Do not sign a document you have not read in full.

STEP 3

Call before you attend

Tell us the agency, the date on the invitation and the office named. We will tell you what to expect and whether we can attend with you.

The firm

Kelvin Iorzenda & Associates is a full-service law firm headquartered in Makurdi, Benue State. It was registered in 2018 and practises across commercial law, contracts, land, landlord and tenant matters, criminal defence and notarial work.

The principal, Kelvin Iorzenda, Esq., is a Barrister and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria and a Notary Public of Nigeria. He served as Secretary of the Nigerian Bar Association, Makurdi Branch, from June 2024 to June 2026, and has spoken publicly on the implementation of Benue State's Administration of Criminal Justice Law.
