Principal Partner

Kumater Kelvin Iorzenda, Esq.

(Notary Public)
Principal Partner

Kelvin was called to the Nigerian Bar as a Barrister and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria on 14th February, 2012. He has over 15 years of experience in core litigation, courtroom practice and public interest advocacy. He is the Benue State Lead of the Digital Rights Lawyers Initiative (DRLI).

His work focuses on providing legal representation to individuals and corporate clients, including — but not limited to — defending victims of unlawful detention and fundamental rights violations. He has litigated cases under the Fundamental Rights (Enforcement Procedure) Rules across Benue, Nasarawa, the FCT and the Federal High Courts on issues including torture, gender-based violence, freedom of expression and discrimination.

As technology evolves, Kelvin is deepening his expertise in digital rights, data protection, privacy and online freedoms.

He is a regular legal analyst on Benue Television, NTA, Channels TV, News Central, AIT, Brothers FM, Agate Radio and several other stations and media outlets, where he provides legal insights on a wide range of trending and complex legal issues — including, but not limited to, aviation and consumer rights, social media freedom and constitutional governance.
